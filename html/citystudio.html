CityStudio

2025

Context

CityStudio partners with Simon Fraser University to provide students with hands-on, real-world projects. These initiatives give students valuable experience while fostering innovation within the City of Vancouver. Our goal: improve engagement and interaction between students, instructors, and CityStudio.

My Contributions

  • Served as primary client liaison, scheduling meetings, sending biweekly updates, and facilitating collaboration across the 10-week project.
  • Led research into the problem space, identifying where interactions were lacking and uncovering the true bottleneck in engagement.
  • Designed concept visuals and graphics for the microsite, and validated a physical prototype.
  • Coordinated communication between the team and client to align expectations and project goals.

Research & Insights

Through surveys and interviews with students, we discovered that awareness of CityStudio was low, but students supported its mission. Deeper research revealed the real engagement gap was between CityStudio and SFU instructors, not students. Instructors act as the bridge, so improving their engagement drives student participation.

Design Challenge

The biggest challenge was convincing CityStudio to shift focus from students to instructors. Evidence had to show that enhancing instructor engagement would directly increase student involvement.

Solution: CityStudio Integration Program

Interactive Physical Prototype

Design Rationale

The physical mailer was designed to stand out, be noticed, and allow instructors to engage at their own pace without feeling forced. The microsite provided interactive content and progressive disclosure, offering a detailed 13-week course timeline while keeping updates easy to maintain.

Outcome & Impact

By reframing the engagement challenge to target instructors, we created a sustainable, low-cost strategy that increased instructor awareness and facilitated smoother student participation. CityStudio recognized the value of research-backed insights in driving strategic decisions.

Reflection

This project taught me the importance of client communication and negotiation. I learned how to reframe assumptions, present evidence effectively, and build consensus for design solutions that address the true problem.

Key Takeaways

1. Engagement problems often stem from the wrong target audience

2. Low-tech solutions can outperform digital ones when aligned with real behavior

3. Effective client communication can reshape problem framing
